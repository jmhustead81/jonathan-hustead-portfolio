# Jonathan Hustead

Staff Software Engineer in Jacksonville, Florida.

I build front-end systems that stay fast and usable as products grow. Most of my work sits between design intent and production-ready UI.

## About

I am a Staff Software Engineer with more than twenty years in front-end development. In my current role I own much of the UI architecture work, which means setting patterns other teams can reuse and keeping complex products coherent as they evolve. The goal is usually the same: pages that feel quick and hold up across devices, with accessibility treated as part of the build rather than a late pass.

Day to day I work in .NET Core, Razor Class Libraries, MVC, and micro-frontend setups when a monolith needs room to breathe. I have an M.S. in Management Information Systems from UCF. Most of my career has been spent on product UI in financial services and healthcare, with earlier years on large-scale e-commerce.
